SWEET CITY · 甜蜜之城

A walkable, fully procedural candy town (three.js): frosted rooftops, soft-serve towers, candy-cane lamps, a river with a striped bridge, traffic that stops at red lights, and a full day–night cycle. Everything is generated in your browser.
可漫步的程序化甜品小城：糖霜屋顶、冰淇淋塔楼、拐杖糖路灯、河上的条纹大桥、会等红灯的车流和完整的昼夜循环，全部在浏览器里实时生成。
